Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Before We Get Started?

Everything you may want to know before starting a website, AI Agent or digital project with IM Systems.

How long does it take to build a website?
It depends on the size and complexity of the project. A simple website and a larger custom website require different timelines. Before the project begins, we'll review the scope with you and confirm the expected delivery process.
Do I need to pay everything upfront?
The completed work is presented for review before final payment. Once the agreed project is reviewed and approved, final payment is completed. Any project-specific payment terms are confirmed with you before work begins.
Can you redesign my existing website?
Yes. We can review your current website, identify what should be improved and determine whether a redesign or a complete rebuild makes more sense for your business and goals.
What can the AI Agent do?
The AI Agent can answer customer questions, explain your services, capture leads, qualify enquiries, collect customer information and guide visitors toward booking, WhatsApp or direct contact with your team.
Can the AI Agent answer customers 24/7?
Yes. Once configured for your business, the AI Agent can assist website visitors at any time and answer common questions based on the information and rules provided for your business.
Can the AI Agent send customers to WhatsApp?
Yes. The experience can include WhatsApp handoff so visitors who want to speak with a person can move directly from the AI conversation to your WhatsApp contact.
Do you work with international clients?
Yes. IM Systems works remotely and the service is designed for businesses in different countries. Communication, project reviews and delivery can all be handled online.
What do you need from me to start?
We normally start with information about your business, your goals, the services or products you offer, the pages or features you need and any brand material or design references you already have.
What if I don't know which package I need?
That's completely fine. Tell us what you're trying to achieve and the features you need. We can then help you understand which option fits the project instead of choosing only by package name.
Will the website work properly on mobile?
Yes. Our websites are designed to adapt across desktop, tablet and mobile so the experience stays clear and usable across different screen sizes.
Can you connect forms, WhatsApp and social media?
Yes. Depending on the project, we can integrate enquiry forms, WhatsApp links, social channels and other contact points so visitors have a clear path to reach your business.
What happens after the website is completed?
You'll review the completed project and request any agreed adjustments. Once approved, the project can move to launch and we'll confirm any handover, support or maintenance included in your project.
Still have a question? Ask our AI Assistant or talk to us directly on WhatsApp.
